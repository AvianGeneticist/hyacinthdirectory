Capitol Overview

Components 1246 (Mechanized Archons)
Durability 38/10
Strength Physical: 12/10
Psionic: 16/10
Magical: 7/10
Specials Spinal Laser
Large Module Support (x24)
Personality Flamboyant

Capitols are titanic Analogues that are assembled in parallel with regular titans when a Mechanized Archon is spawning titans. They act as hubs for Serf and Division activity, and also possess some significant firepower via large energy weapons mounted through their maw. A group of Capitols is called a center.

Physical Description

Capitols are substantially larger than any other Analogue, standing at 84' (2560cm) tall. Their Haetin bodies are also quite heavily built, though notably a fair amount of their interiors are hollow to facilitate living and working space inside. The insides of a Capitol are not spacious enough for anything bigger than an Archon, but they provide space enough for storage of spare resources or transport of smaller puppets.

The defining traits of a Capitol are no doubt its large rings, which it uses as the basis for all of its enlarged modules. These body rings prevent the Capitol from laying down, though this is rarely an issue for them. Each ring lazily spins in a different direction, and which ring spins which way is considered a Capitol's "handedness".

Behaviour

Capitols primarily act as large central hubs for large quantities of Serfs and Divisions housed in their enlarged modules; said modules have roughly 20x the working capacity, so a Capitol can host a staggering number of subpuppets for various functions. Some modules such as Graspers are not translated to their increased size, and as such a Capitol's range of roles tends to be a bit more limited, making up for this fact with sheer brute force. Notably, one module exclusive to the Capitol is a specialized thruster, allowing the Capitol to maneuver even in a vacuum. This allows them to be deployed in space from a Leviathan, though the structural strain is generally too high for them to take off within a gravity well.

In addition to their modules, Capitols host a central, spinal energy weapon that runs from their tail out through their mouths. This weapon, while a significantly power laser, only tops out in energy output similar to that of a Perditioner's weakest attacks, and as such, it is typically used defensively to protect from smaller projectiles or adversaries. The Capitol does have much more control over its beam than a Perditioner, however, so it sees usage in industrial (mining) applications.

Genetic Description

Capitols, as Analogues, have no sort of genetic makeup. However, their modules are all generally patterned after that of more standard Mechanized Puppet modules, and the actual weapon part of their spinal lasers are similar to that of a Spinogun, just much larger. It is believed that other titan Analogues would use similar oversized modules, though sadly no such example of this exists.

Metanarrative

Capitols are just giant robots. I wish I could say there was more to them than that, but that's really what they boil down to. They do take some other design inspirations with things like the body rings and spinal mount laser cannon, but really it all boils back to "beeg robits r fun nd cool". I just wanted to make a titan Analogue, so I did, and sometimes that's all you need.
