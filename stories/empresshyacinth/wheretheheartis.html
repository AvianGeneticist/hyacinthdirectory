Hyacinth took a deep breath. "Ester, could you come with me for a moment?" They asked.

Ester blinked, setting Solace down. "Alright mother," She replied softly. "This one will be right back," She promised her sister, briefly waving to Cardion, Solace, and Allium, following Hyacinth out of the room.

Hyacinth led Ester to the room across the hall; it had laid unused for quite some time, and now felt a good place for a private talk.

Ester felt her heart sink. Her mind raced with thoughts of what this could be about. She tried to prepare herself, thinking of how she could react, what lies or cover stories she could spin.

Hyacinth shut the door once they and Ester were inside, taking a seat.

Ester looked up at them nervously, refusing to sit down.


"Now, Ester..." Hyacinth started. "I didn't want to force you out like this. I was hoping you'd feel comfortable enough to talk to me about it on your own eventually. But it hurts to see you feel so unwelcome around us," They expressed.

Ester's ears pinned back. "Wh-what?" She asked nervously.

"We know you're a changeling, Ester," Hyacinth answered, leaving nothing to uncertainty.

Ester tensed. Her lip quivered. "H-how?" She questioned, unable to ask anything else as tears pricked at her eyes.

Hyacinth frowned gently, pulling Ester into a hug. They didn't address her question right away. "It's okay, dear. Let it out," They expressed softly.

Ester broke into ugly cries, torn between trying to run away and sinking into the only source of comfort she'd ever known. Her posture faltered and she slumped against Hyacinth.

"It's okay..." Hyacinth gently stroked Ester's mane, nuzzling the top of her head gently. "You're not in trouble," They promised.


Ester sobbed into Hyacinth, clinging to them. Months of fear and tensions came out all at once. It took her a long time to bring herself to some sort of composure, looking up at Hyacinth and whimpering.

"Does that feel better, dear?" Hyacinth asked tenderly.

Ester looked up. "Th-this one doesn't understand... M-mother hates changelings," She whimpered.

"Ah..." Hyacinth's ears drooped a bit. "It is true that we have history with changelings. We've had to defend ourselves against them in the past. But you're also my daughter, and I love you. Just being a changeling doesn't make you any lesser," They expressed gently.

Ester blinked. "But this one was made to infiltrate..." She trailed off, having trouble looking Hyacinth in the eye.

"You love us, do you not?" Hyacinth asked gently. "Me, Cardion, Solace, Allium..." They trailed of.

"This one does," Ester nodded.

"Then it doesn't matter what you were made for. Just what you chose to do," Hyacinth promised with a gentle smile. "You're a part of our family, Ester."


Ester took a deep breath, pulling herself away from Hyacinth. Without a word, she finally allowed her disguise to drop. Pink fur gave way to dark chitin, though curiously, the mane remained, just decidedly more styled than the usual loose, frayed cut Hyacinth wore.

Hyacinth smiled, gently tracing Ester's chin with a hoof. "You look lovely, dear," They promised gently.

Ester managed a weak smile for a moment, before it faced. "What will the others think?" She asked softly.

"You're still a part of our family. I know they'll accept you," Hyacinth promised, before putting two of their front hooves on Ester's shoulders. "And if anyone else gives you trouble for being a changeling, tell me. I will put them right," They insisted seriously.

"Yes mother," Ester replied softly, leaning forward to hug Hyacinth again.

Hyacinth smiled, giving Ester a good squeeze before letting her go to stand up. "Now, let's go tell your siblings. I wanted to talk to you in private first," They expressed, offering Ester a hoof.


Ester took Hyacinth's hoof. "Oh, but mother... This one's spawn are also changelings."

"That's alright, dear," Hyacinth smiled. "Perhaps working alongside the others will be easier now that they don't have to worry about hiding," They winked, beginning to lead Ester across the hall again.

Ester blinked, but nodded. She closed her eyes for a moment, sending the signal to her siblings. There weren't yet too many of them, but those that were were finally free to be themselves. She could feel the relief; thousands of intangible muscles relaxing as the necessity to hide was lifted.

Hyacinth shuddered. "Even I could feel that..." They murmured. "You have all been hiding for a long time," They frowned gently.

"Yes..." Ester admitted. "It is not hard to hold disguises, but it does become strenuous after a long time."

"Well, you don't have to worry about that anymore," Hyacinth promised with a gentle smile, opening the door to their room.


Those in the room (Cardion, Allium, Solace, and Archie) looked up at Hyacinth and Ester as they returned. They all, of course, instinctively knew it was Ester, but that didn't stop some confusion from cropping up on Cardion's face.

"Is Ester okay?" Cardion asked, standing up to approach Ester with curious eyes.

Ester managed a bare smile. "Yes... This one is better than this has ever been," She expressed.

"Ester has been nervous about showing us her true self for a long time," Hyacinth explained. "I don't want her to have to hide anymore."


Archie stood up, slowly blinking as she slightly wobbled on her way over, eyes beginning to glow. "...Finally comes the inner beauty..." She said slowly.

Ester hesitated. "Thas one knew?" She asked.

"...Curators must know all things..." Archie replied, awkwardly slumping against Ester in a hug.

Ester did her best to hold Archie up, returning the hug. "This one is glad you're not upset."

"...Curiosities are welcome..." Archie replied. "...One day hopes to understand the great mind..."

Ester blushed a little at that comment, but nodded. "Thank thas," She replied softly.


Ester's gaze turned downward as a pink blur smacked into her leg. Solace looked up at her with a slightly dazed expression, plopped back on her rump. Ester smiled, carefully shifting Archie to one side to scoop up the filly, doing so with some difficulty. "Hello sister," She greeted with a soft smile.

"Efter!" Solace bopped Ester on the snout, making the larger archon squeak.

"Ah..." Hyacinth paused, before smiling. "It seems like you're going to be her first word," Their expressed with a soft giggle.

"Solace..." Ester nuzzled Solace gently, a few tears pricking at her eyes again. "Thas one makes this one feel like this was always meant to be here," She murmured softly.
