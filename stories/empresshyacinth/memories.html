"Thas one feels familiar," Cardion expressed, looking over Archie with a puzzled expression.

"That's because she is your... Aunt, in a manner of speaking," Hyacinth explained, looking at the strange being next to them.

Archie nodded. "...Memories given form..." She attempted to explain. "...Mind maintains the body maintains the mind..." Her legs quivered.

Gently, Hyacinth helped Archie onto the bed so she could lay down. "Here, dear. I know this is unfamiliar to you," They expressed.


"Is there something wrong with thos?" Ester asked.

"...Weight... More than normal..." Archie replied.

"This one can carry thas if desired," Cardion offered.

Archie smiled. "...Kind..." She expressed, allowing her head to rest on the bed. "...Thank you..."

"I believe she just needs some time to get a hang of things, my dears," Hyacinth expressed as they sat on the bed next to Archie. "If my theory is right, she's not used to having weight."

"All of these ones have weight," Cardion tilted her head. "How does thos one not?"

"It is difficult to explain..." Hyacinth admitted. "You remember what I told you about your roots, right? How they go elsewhere, and eventually you no longer have to carry them?"

Cardion nodded, tilting her head in the other direction.

It was Ester who put it together. "Archie comes from the place with no weight," She mused.

"Exactly," Hyacinth smiled. "I believe Archie has been there for a long time, but only recently have I become... Aware of her," They expressed. "When I stare off into space, it's me trying to see her again."


Solace had crawled up onto the bed by now, cuddling up against Archie's side. Archie gently draped some of her top half over the Archon, smiling gently. "...Brave child..." She expressed contently.

Cardion watched, walking over to sit near Archie and her mother. "It is nice to meet thas one," She expressed. "This one's name is Cardion."

Archie turned back to Cardion, smiling gently. "...Knows thy name..." She replied. "...Memories... The mind..."

"Oh," Cardion blinked. "How does thas one have so many memories?" She asked.

Archie took a deep breath. "...Curates the mind... Tends to every memory... Every puppet... Every fiber... Prunes away the damage and growing pain..." She attempted to explain. "...Silent caretaker..."

Hyacinth gave a weak smile. "You always did take care of trees the best," They murmured. "But, ah... That is important work, is it not? You will have to return soon."

"...Body knows what it needs..." Archie replied, looking up at Hyacinth. "...New curator will join... Looking forward to not being alone..."


"A new curator?" Ester asked. "Are there more of thas?"

Archie was quiet for a long moment. "...Not yet..." She admitted. "...This one came from needs... Patterns in damage and health... Another will come soon... The body knows..."

"Oh..." Ester blinked. Her ears drooped slightly. She wondered if Archie knew she lie she was living. She wondered if Hyacinth knew. Ester took a deep breath, trying to remain calm.

Hyacinth looked at Ester. "Ester, dear... You look scared," They expressed gently, patting the bed next to them. "Come here, dear."

Ester sheepishly padded over, sitting next to Hyacinth, who draped an arm over her and pulled her close. "It's okay," Hyacinth promised gently. "Mother will make it all better."

"This one knows," Ester replied softly, leaning into Hyacinth and closing her eyes, taking another deep breath.


Archie looked up at Hyacinth. "...Intruding?" She asked.

"No, dear. It's just late, and we're all about ready for bed," Hyacinth replied. "You're welcome to stay with us tonight, or return to the mind where you're more comfortable. Though I suppose you'll be with us either way," They mused with a soft chuckle.

Archie slowly blinked. "...Staying sounds nice..." She admitted. "...But must go to greet the new curator... Many things are not easy to learn..."

"Ah..." Hyacinth nodded, ears drooping a bit. "Well, thank you for taking your time to introduce yourself to us, dear. We'll take good care of that body so you can use it when you'd like," They promised.

Archie smiled. "...Will always be here, only slightly... Will be able to watch," She promised. Her eyes closed for a long moment as the glowing part of her body dimmed. When she opened them again, no glow in her eyes remained, and she looked around neutrally before laying her head down to sleep.

Hyacinth gently rubbed Archie's head. "Rest well, dear," They expressed gently.



Archie was back in the familiar place they knew. The colossal tree that made up Hyacinth's body, branches spreading out in all directions. Thick roots laid below, tied to each Archon, save for a curious green shoot sticking up near the base. She gently rubbed the odd little shoot like a beloved animal, wanting to ensure it knew it was wanted. Archie also noticed a thin, vinelike tether binding her to her physical body. She tested that it stretched and flexed as easily as everything else, before shifting to begin her rounds of the enormous tree that made up her world


It didn't take her long to find who she was looking for. A new creature like herself, wrapped frantically around one of the thick trunks. This creature resembled her, with the curious addition of a horn and wings. She slid up carefully, introducing herself. She paused to allow a response: Twilight Sparkle.

Archie explained to Twilight Sparkle that she was not actually Twilight Sparkle, the same way that Archie was not Arched Trellis. Archie was impressed at how quickly Twilight Sparkle grasped the concept: It had taken Archie a long time to fully understand it. Archie took note that Twilight Sparkle was likely someone intelligent, and thus the memory followed.


Then a different reply came; a request. A desire to be called "Sparks", as Twilight Sparkle was, in fact, not Twilight Sparkle, and if Twilight Sparkle did exist, another name would be needed to tell them apart.

Archie agreed with this, pondering that for a moment. She wondered if she, too, should take on a new name. But it was clear that Archie being Archie was important to Hyacinth, so Archie left it for now. After all, she had a more important job to do: Showing Sparks the rules of this new place.


Sparks listened intently as Archie explained the rules of this new place to her: Moving ana and kata, following branches, and the all-important task of pruning away the dead ends of unused fibers. It took Sparks a few tries to get it right, but importantly, she always practiced very safe practices; always taking too little, rather than too much.

Archie gave her approval and gestured out to the vastness of the tree. She explained how to travel, and that there would always be new work, as the body and mind continued to grow. She also explained the trunk to Sparks, that Hyacinth was there, and that she could enter the physical world if needed. Sparks gave an appreciative reply, before the two curators went their separate ways to begin their work.
