While most ponies know that puppets lack genders of any kind, this fact is baked into their language, too

Archie tells me that puppets have six major pronouns which focus on subject clusivity and plurality:

SINGULAR

- "This" describes the self and only the self, like how I use... Well, "I". It can also be pronounced "thes" to better match its plural, though this pattern doesn't seem particularly common in any speaking caste. I think I've caught Cardion use it once or twice when speaking quickly.

- "Thas" refers to the single entity the self is speaking to, comparable to "you". In a two way conversation, you'll probably hear this a lot. It's interesting they pick a vowel that wasn't used in our own tongue when transcribing it over...

- "Thos" refers to a singular entity that is not the speaker or the one being addressed. He, she, and it all get kind of squashed into one word due to the aforementioned lack of genders. Another word that doesn't seem to be borrowed from Ponish in any way, though in this case it's easy to see they took the plural and worked backwards.

PLURAL

- "These" refers to a group that the self is a part of, analogous to "we" or "us". Puppets lack any kind of formal or royal we; if a title is important, it is used instead (for example, Hyacinth is always "mother", though weirdly Archons seem to be less consistently referred to as such).

- "Thase" refers to a group the self is addressing. Ponish doesn't have an explicit plural form of you, but if it did, this would be comparable (I think Prench has one?)


- "Those" refers to a group that doesn't include the speaker nor the one being addressed. "They" is close enough here, though with a particular emphasis on the assumption of indeterminate gender in this case.

All of the above pronouns can be sufficed with "one/ones" as appropriate. There doesn't seem to be hard or established grammar in regards to this - it takes into account the speaker's personal preference, the sentence's flow, and to a lesser extent the urgency of the message. Given puppets all intuitively know everything important that's conveyed between them, it's sensible enough.
