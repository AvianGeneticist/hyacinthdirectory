Hitch Overview

Commonality 6.8% (Warped Archons)
Durability 7/10
Strength Physical: 3/10
Psionic: 3/10
Tapestral: 5/10
Specials Maintenance of reality
Limited flight
Personality Responsible and naggy

Hitches are somewhat small heavy puppets created by Warped Archons, and act as the regulators and enforcers of reality. They help repair damage left behind by edits and can create safe zones for more dangerous experiments. A group of Hitches is called a frame.

Variants

Reconcilants - Howitzers - Mesosae - Haulers - Hitches

Physical Description

Hitches are on the smaller side for heavy puppets, standing at a meager 4' 7" (140cm). Their smaller frames, however, aid them in flight, which is achieved by their panel-like wings. Hitches do not sport particularly complex bodies otherwise, and unusually for warped puppets they have no spinnerettes of any kind. Their bodies are surprisingly robust thanks to this simplicity, with more room for spare organs and thicker hide.

Behaviour

Hitches fulfill an incredibly important role in the reality altering process: Safety. Their primary purpose is repairing the damage done caused by edits to reality, mitigating any long term damage to an absolute minimum. They can also act as preventative measures for long-term damage, with their wings being able to project connectable boundaries that can section off portions of space for use in more dangerous experiments. They fulfill a dichotomy with Looms in this regard, being the wardens to the Looms' wilier sides.

Notably, when setting up safe zones, Hitches will work closely with Seraphs to ensure all puppets involved with an experiment are recovered in the event of dangerous outcomes. Their psionics seem focused on tying themselves to other puppets, ensuring there is a direct link that can be recovered in the event of emergencies.

Hitches also have rather parental personalities, and can be often seen actively nagging other puppets to ensure safe work practices are followed, even for puppets other than Looms. While this occasionally comes to the annoyance of certain, more mischievous puppet types, it is generally perceived as an overall positive for the clan.

Genetic Description

Hitches sport the unusual genetics common to warped puppets, but with extra genes to account for their ability to fly. They also have genes related to their durability, particularly in the skin; this is presumably to ensure they are not damaged during their wardening duties, though the effectiveness of their ability to resist reality altering events through brute force is up for debate.

Hyacinth's Thoughts

I suppose I've never considered how important safety measures have been to my family. The Deltas have been protecting us for a long time, the Praetors ensure no one is left defenseless in the case of bad actors... And of course the Seraphs can fix anything that's otherwise beyond repair. I suppose the Hitches are just an extension of the desire to ensure all of my children are protected, no matter how unruly they are.

Metanarrative

Being the middle child of an already somewhat generic line doesn't really do Haulers any favours, so extra attention was put on their personality; hence their increased sense of empathy and greater responsiveness to the gestalt's needs. It allows me to develop their character more without needing expressly designed elements.
