Labyrinth Overview

Components 7865
Durability 37/10
Strength Physical: 21/10
Psionic: 34/10
Magical: 0/10
Specials Internal space folding
Reality Manipulation
Translation of different rules of reality
Personality Slightly scatterbrained

Labyrinths are colossal titans capped off by a Warped Archon. They act as mobile research facilities, either able to alter large areas around them with their vast quanitities of spinnerettes or facilitate research in a contained environment within their space-folded innards. A group of Labyrinths is called an abyss.

Variants

Leviathans - Labyrinths

Physical Description

Labyrinths are quite a bit smaller than the usual Leviathans they share a body template with, removing their elongated tails and reducing their height down to 460' (14020cm). This is compensated, however, for vastly increased internal complexity: A few small orifices give access to an incredible amount of space, approximating the total internal volume of a Plurite, organized into multiple large atriums connected by more modestly-sized tunnels. This internal complexity allows the Labyrinth to contain an incredible amount of material, which is particularly useful for performing experiments that would be too dangerous to perform in open space.

Labyrinths also carry on their sides an enormous abundance of detachable heads and spinnerettes, allowing them to perform wide scale edits even outside of their own bodies. They have an incredible capacity for focusing on multiple subjects at once, and it is not uncommon for a single Labyrinth to maintain an inordinate amount of edits all on its own.

Behaviour

Labyrinths are purpose-built to be walking research bases, both internally for experimentation and externally for deployment of researched techniques. Their insides are hardened and able to be sealed for the sake of researching, with internal partitions allowing several large-scale experiments to be conducted at once. While not their intended purpose, it is also possible for the internal spaces to be used for regular farming or housing, and given their incredible size it is not uncommon for a Labyrinth to at least provide some of these services.

Labyrinths provide a crucial ability that no other warped puppet can: Translation. A Labyrinth can cleanly adjust matter and energy from one reality's rules to another, accounting for different rules and regulations. While not universally useful, it is a necessity when working with many different exotic technologies; particularly technologies that allow a Labyrinth to produce caloric energy to distribute around the gestalt.

Given their penchant for focusing on multitasking, it comes as little surprise that Labyrinths often display somewhat scatterbrained behaviour, often juggling many tasks at the same time. This does not typically cause them stress, but given the occasionally delicate nature of their work, it is occasionally necessary to intervene in their workflow collecting habits.

Genetic Description

Labyrinths share a mix of warped puppet peculiarity and much of the solidity already found in Leviathans. It is believed the hardening of the cellular structure is critical to the Labyrinth's success, hence why they cannot be made a variant of any typical titan. Otherwise, Labyrinths do not display any unusual genetic behaviour.

Hyacinth's Thoughts

Labyrinths are so strange to fully process. They appear somewhat normal on the outside, if large by titan standards, but their internal size is truly colossal. If they came about earlier, perhaps all of us could have fit in a single one, never to be bothered by the outside world again. Now, however, they are simply a very helpful tool for mass transport and experimentation; one we can all appreciate.

Metanarrative

Labyrinths represent the first step into nascent universe creation: Still fully enclosed within a puppet, but representing a colossal amount of folded space. They were originally going to have more conventional "pocket dimension to a single planet" or similar ability, but I prefer the idea that their insides are some kind of giant enclosed facility. It gives them more to differentiate themselves from the Bathypelae.
