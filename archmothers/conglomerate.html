Archmother Overview

Definition An Archmother with stunted psionics
Durability ??/10
Strength Physical: 4/10
Psionic: ??/10
Magical: 7/10

Spellcasting
Personality Variable; leans toward parental

Conglomerated Archmothers, or just Conglomerates, are failed Archmothers who cannot develop psionic outreach. While their bodies are capable of (and in fact, particularly good at) spawning, they are incapable of forming (at any noticeable scale) psionic bonds with puppets, and as such cannot organize a network. A group of Conglomerates is known as a slough.

Relatives

Parents: N/A

Children: Manipulators, Tricksters, Deltas, Myrmecophs, Reconcilants, Howitzers, Mesosae, Praetors, Totems, Anchorages (with one Serf apiece), Perditioners, Cambrians, Leviathans, non-False Archons

Variants

Archmothers - Conglomerates

Physical Description

Due to their emphasis on internal bodily structure, it comes as little surprise that Conglomerates are much larger than regular Archmothers: They stand at around 10' 2" (309.9cm), with overly bulky builds, particularly around their limbs. They bear all of the basic limb structure that regular Archmothers do (sporting three arms and five legs), but their bodies are completely and utterly festooned with spawning orifices, to the point where a substantial mound of such orifices grows from their back.

Conglomerates also have two limbs that seem to be tipped with proto-Archons, due to having irises but no pupils. These proto-Archons are incapable of being safely extracted from the Archmother, and it is believed that their presence is partially responsible for the sheer bulk spawning Conglomerates are capable of.

Behaviour

Conglomerates are utterly superlative at spawning, as their appearance and internal biology implies. In a single spawning cycle, a Conglomerate may release up to two dozen pupae in a single pulse, with the recursive nature of their psionics meaning that a single spawning event is repeated as many as six times. Conglomerates are also capable of passive spawning, with a small amount of pupae occasionally "leaking" from various orifices.

Sadly, without support, a Conglomerate's spawning abilities largely go to waste, as a Conglomerate is incapable of connecting to its puppets' roots. Most puppets die immediately, but others will exist in an "undead" state for a short time as their bodies fully succumb to disconnection.

It is possible, however, for a Conglomerate to be "adopted" by a healthy Archmother. This process involves the joining of the two psionic networks, in which the Conglomerate takes on a role similar to an Archon. An adopted Conglomerate typically has higher authority than Archons in regards to organizing and controlling the gestalt, but still cannot supercede the will of the adopting Archmother.

While it is by no means their primary capability, Conglomerates also show vastly different magical abilities relative to normal Archmothers. In particular, a Conglomerate cannot focus on as many separate spell tasks at one time, but has substantially increased magical output.

Genetic Description

Conglomerates display many of the same triggers that Archmothers do, though much of their bodies are inundated with spawning tissues, even in place of muscle or nerves in some cases. This anatomy does explain their extreme proclivity for spawning, though there is little to suggest that their biology is at fault for their inability to maintain puppets.

Known Spells

Conglomerates share much of their magical abilities with conventional Archmothers. However, some of their spells are seemingly deliberate alterations of more conventional ones. Their observed spells are listed here for convenience:

  • Psychokinesis: Conglomerates seemingly lack the coordination to structure their psychokinesis into hands, but make up for it with sheer brute force. A Conglomerate's psychokinetic abilities are considerably strong, to the point where some Conglomerates levitate themselves to allow them to run faster or leap great distances.
  • Magic Waves: Broad blasts of forceful magic that form the backbone of a Conglomerate's self-defense strategy. While not the most offensive of spells, the amount of force imparted is enough to send most creatures and projectiles back from whence they came.
  • Light Crash: A deluge of light magic unleashed from the Conglomerate's horn. While not elegant by any means, the resulting blast of light can leave an area brightly lit for a long time.
  • Healing Magic: Surprisingly, a Conglomerate's healing magic is largely intact, with minimal differences in overall ability. However, a Conglomerate is decidedly more suited to projecting large amounts of healing energy over precisely fixing a single target.

Hyacinth's Thoughts

Looking at a Conglomerate is... A bit like looking into a broken mirror, I'm sorry to say. I would never judge them for what happened to them, of course, but there is a sense of uncanniness that I can't shake when I look at them. I suppose I just find them offputting, more than anything.

That said, of course, I would never say no to one looking for psionic support. I can't imagine what it's like to loose a child, just to have it slip through your hooves as quickly as it came to you. It's a fate I wouldn't wish on anyone.

Metanarrative

Conglomerates play on the idea of a "failed clone" of an Archmother, with the typical shtick of "being bred for one thing without realizing that all the others are important". Hence, they go all in on puppet spawning, to where they can't really make use of all of it by themselves.
