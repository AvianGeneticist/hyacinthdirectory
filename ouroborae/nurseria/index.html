Nurseria are pangeanic Ouroborae of significant size. While their body plan is not particularly exotic, they host large, hollow landmasses with active lithospheres for the purpose of spawning Progenites. A group of Nurseria is called a protosphere.

Physical Description

Nurseria are fairly large Ouroborae, standing at roughly 3442 miles (5539.4km) tall. Their height is further inflated by the structure of their landmasses, which tend to rise upwards to make room for the large chambers that define a Nurserian's appearance. This chamber is incredibly important, as it is a safe place where a Nurserian can grow its Progenites, and a Nurserian will often request the assistance of other Ouroborae or a Perditioner should it become blocked. Otherwise, however, Nurseria are rather nondescript, though like Regalids they lack any sort of wing structure for aligning themselves with a world shell.

Behaviour

Nurseria act like giant parallels to Reconcilants, being relatively nondescript in and of themselves but being defined by their ability to spawn smaller creatures. Of course, in Nurseria, this spawning produces subouroborae rather than subpuppets, however the basic principle remains the same: The Nurserian handles all the biological and psionic overhead of creating the new subouroboran, before eventually expelling it as a distinct entity. The spawning process of a Nurseria is, however, quite a bit slower and more gradual as the Nurserian both builds the body and assembles the continent of its new spawn. In all, it generally takes about 6 years to create a new subouroboran, which is incredibly fast by Ouroboran standards.

With their bodies so defined by their ability to spawn, it comes as little surprise that Nurseria do not have much else going for them. While they are unable to easily move amongst a planet shell, they do possess the ability to provide an anchor point for their subouroborae, allowing them to move freely in a significant radius around the Nurserian.

Nurseria, also like Reconcilants, show strong parental personalities, with tender movements and affectionate gestures even towards other large Ouroborae. In particular, they are one of the few Ouroborae types that Striates will actively seek out as neighbors, as Nurseria and their subouroborae can provide a healthy buffer with minimal risk of damaging collisions.

Genetic Description

Nurseria bare no especially strong epigenetic resemblance to any Ouroboran, aside from the common features of a hybrid bio-tectonic organism. They instead share particular epigenetic complexities with Reconcilants, and it is believed that Nurseria emerge for a similar reason that Reconcilants do: The rapid proliferation and management of smaller agents for the clan.

Hyacinth's Thoughts

I suppose it shouldn't surprise me that, eventually, one of the Ouroborae would also become a mother of its own. Despite this, I was quite surprised the first time I saw a little Progenite being born. The Nurseria are wonderful mothers; ones I can be proud of. It makes me content to know that my influence can affect even the largest among us.

Metanarrative

Nurseria are basically just big Reconcilants. They spawn a more mobile flier unit, are dedicated to spawning and support, and just are generally big chonksters with an emphasis on being all gentle and maternal. The biggest surprise is that they weren't part of the first round of Ouroborae; what took me so long?
