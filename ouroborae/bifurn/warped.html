Bathypelan Overview

Commonality 15.3% (Warped Puppet Accumulation)
Stability 7/10
Attributes Synchronicity: 6/10
Activity: 0/10
Peculiarity: 10/10
Specials Nascent universe anchoring
Personality Claustrophobic

Bathypelan Ouroborae are very special Ouroborae that uncommonly arise when large quantities of Warped Puppets live on a landmass. They do not host traditional continents, but instead contain entire nascent universes within them. A group of Bathypelae is known as a bang.

Variants

Bifurn - Bathypelae

Physical Description

Bathypelae are on the larger side for continental Ouroborae, standing at roughly 3268 miles (5259.3km) tall. Compared to other Ouroborae, they have relatively simple body plans, aside from the large six large tusks on their backs that form the gateways between the outside world and the nascent universes they carry. They also have the typical Ouroboran wings for adjusting their spots in a world shell, which is especially important for Bathypelae, who like to have some amount of space between them and others.

Behaviour

Bathypelae are known for their nascent universes: Pocket dimensions roughly 1000 light years across accessed by one of seven gateways that hang between the tusks on their back. While externally these are fixed structures, the exit point inside the nascent universe can be moved or rotated to the gestalt's liking, allowing for unprecedented levels of flexibility and development.

While most Bathypelae will be used for experimentation (given they offer an entire fresh, malleable universe that can be safely observed from the outside), they also have a practical use in macro-scale housing. A nascent universe can be populated with stars and planets, eventually turning into what is essentially a dwarf galaxy located on-world. It is believed that other Bathypelae could exist inside one, as although most Ouroborae are too large to pass through the entrances, it is possible to grow them within a Bathypelan's nascent universe, giving a puppet clan effectively unlimited space.

Genetic Description

Bathypelae show much of the weirdness found by other puppets related to Warped Archons, but otherwise simply showcase a much higher density than other Ouroborae. This is assumed to help them weather the forces involved with maintaining a pocket dimension; a task the Bathypelae seem to have little issue with.

Hyacinth's Thoughts

I cannot help but feel prickles under my skin, looking at a Bathypelan. An entire universe to control, even if it is smaller than our home galaxy, is a truly incredible proposition... Nevermind having multiple of them, potentially all able to host their own multitudes of Bathypelae within, each one with their own universes and Bathypelae inside. They do not like being compacted, so we accommodate for them thusly; it's not hard to do when we have effectively limitless space, after all.

Metanarrative

Bathypelae are the first variant Ouroboran, representing the absolute pinnacle of a puppet clan's dominance over reality. While Ouroborae don't exactly come cheap, the rewards are exceptional, representing an endless frontier for Hyacinth to expand into. This is a good thing for them: I imagine the idea of eventually being all that exists terrifying Hyacinth, so having a way to expand forever with minimal destruction would be a great relief to them.
