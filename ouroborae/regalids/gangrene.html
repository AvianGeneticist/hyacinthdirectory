Realminth Overview

Commonality 3.5% (Gangrenous Archon Buildup)
Stability 12/10
Attributes Synchronicity: 3/10
Activity: 1/10
Peculiarity: 6/10
Specials Large scale gravitational bondage
Personality Immovable and rigid

Realminth are abhorrently colossal Ouroborae, responsible for gravitational regulation on a galactic scale. Their colossal bodies often occupy a central world shell with no additional Ouroborae, their own power tempered only by its own unwieldliness. A group of Realminth is known as a yoke.

Variants

Regalids - Realminth

Physical Description

Realminth are truly enormous, even on the scale of Ouroborae: At 10413 miles (16758.1km) tall, they more than double the size of a Regalid, with elongated bodies that carry even more terrain than their size might imply. Their gargantuan frames are marked with gangrenous features such as dark blisters and the edging of their wings being marked with dark fur, and the wings themselves are unusual in design for Ouroborae, trading the emphasized structural "rib" for thick, fleshy membranes that are capable of supporting their own continents.

Behaviour

Realminth exist beyond the scale of regular Ouroborae, capable of forming the basis of a new planet all on their own. They often do just that, existing in a curled posture as the planet's innermost shell layer, arching their bodies and wings to form a roughly spherical shape. This allows a Realminth to act as an anchor for other Ouroborae, providing gravity and an atmosphere to anchor new Ouroborae.

Realminth also act as long-range gravitational tethers, with effective ranges measured in light years. They can connect to one another to project highly concentrated streams of gravitons, allowing them to replicate the attractive effects of galactic core-scale black holes or even outright clusters of dark matter. This does come at a cost, however: Realminth are poorly suited to cooperation. A tangle of crossing gravitational streams is exceedingly dangerous, and requires significant cooperation to ensure no major damage is caused.

Genetic Description

Realminth contain not just size enhancing genes related to those found in Regalids, but also have certain genes related to tumour suppression turned off. It is believed this is how they maintain their colossal size; tissues are allowed to replicate uncontrollably, kept in check by gangrenous energy and the sheer size of the Realminth. Curiously, they are the only known abomination to have this self-regulation mechanism turned off, with even Metastae having self-regulatory mechanisms to ensure their overcoats never eat inwards.

Hyacinth's Thoughts

Regalids were large, but at least made sense on a planetary scale; they could slot into a large ocean with some careful fitting. Realminth almost always need their own section of space away from any other landmasses to settle in. They are indeed so colossal that one could replace our entire homeworld if we so desired.

They do, of course, have their place, acting as the anchors for larger star systems and ensuring orbits and spacing are properly maintained. It is just a shame it is such an ordeal when one springs up without warning.

Metanarrative

Realminth are the epitome of both Ouroboran excess and gangrenous puppets' emphasis on bodily size, strength, and durability. They are somewhat analogous to black holes or other enormous gravity wells, though of course they are still liveable environments.

As a fun fact, the Earth is 12,756 kilometers in diameter, meaning that, if placed next to one another, a Realminth would stand taller than the Earth itself.
